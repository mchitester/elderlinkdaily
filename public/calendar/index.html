📸 Tips for a clean photo

  1. Lay the calendar flat on a table and smooth out the folds.
  2. Stand right above it and hold the phone flat and level, not at an angle.
  3. Get the whole page in the picture, with a little table showing around the edges.
  4. Use good light, like near a window. Turn off the flash and keep your shadow and fingers off the page.
  5. Check that the small print is sharp when you zoom in. If it’s blurry, take it again.
